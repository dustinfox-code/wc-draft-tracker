League standings

Scoring: group win 3 · draw 1 · advance to KO +4 · each KO win +5 · winning the final +10 (5 win + 5 champion bonus). Third-place match is not scored. Expected = mean final total from Silver Bulletin's live odds (banked points included). Likely = the median (50th-percentile) total — the typical outcome. They differ because the points distribution is right-skewed: a small chance of a deep tournament run drags the mean up, so a weak team's Expected can sit well above what it will most likely actually score. A manager's Likely is the median of their roster's combined distribution, so it won't equal the sum of the per-team Likely figures. "vs draft" (Expected view) compares to the pre-tournament expectation. If the odds feed is unreachable the page falls back to the last fetched copy, then to pre-tournament numbers (Likely needs live odds, so it's unavailable in that fallback).
